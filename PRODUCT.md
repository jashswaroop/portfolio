# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (Single-file with GSAP and Swiper.js)

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, clients, and collaborators seeking to hire or partner with an AI Engineer.

## Product Purpose

To showcase Jaswanth Swaroop Kandregula's expertise in AI engineering, RAG systems, LLM infrastructure, and frontend integrations via a high-performance, premium personal portfolio.

## Positioning

A highly interactive, design-focused single-page portfolio that demonstrates both advanced backend AI skills (LLMs, vector databases) and premium frontend polish (custom glassmorphic animations, GSAP marquee).

## Operating Context

Accessed via modern web browsers, evaluated by technical recruiters and engineering managers, often viewed on desktop and mobile screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-file code architecture (`swaroop-portfolio.html`)
- Smooth scroll navigation, light-deceleration skills marquee, interactive hover-expand projects gallery
- Direct contact integrations (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub)

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jaswanth Swaroop Krishna Kandregula
- Visuals: Liquid Glass theme (deep dark theme with glassmorphic cards, specular borders, and colorful ambient light-source gradients)
- Typography: PP Neue Montreal / Plus Jakarta Sans / Outfit

## Evidence on Hand

- Incumbent implementation in [swaroop-portfolio.html](file:///d:/downloads/portfolio/swaroop-portfolio.html)

## Product Principles

- **Impeccable Motion**: Keep all marquee and hover animations jank-free (60FPS) and physics-driven (spring deceleration).
- **Content is King**: Make technical project details clear, readable, and structured, rather than hidden in decoration.
- **Premium Tactility**: Interactive components must feel alive with responsive micro-animations and specular glass highlights.
